Become A Volunteer

Your time and passion can help save street animals in Lucknow.

Why Join Our Volunteer Network?

On-field Rescue Support: Assist our rescue team during emergency calls and animal transportation.

Shelter & Feeding Drives: Help feed and care for recovering dogs, cats, and other rescued animals at our shelter.

Foster Parenting: Provide temporary home care for newborn puppies or recovering animals.

Social Media Support: Help us document and share rescue stories on Instagram and YouTube.
