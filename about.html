About Paws Hopes Society

Learn about our journey, core values, and unwavering commitment to street animal welfare across India.

Our Mission

To provide swift medical response, shelter, nutrition, and rehabilitation for injured, sick, and abandoned animals while promoting adoption over buying.

Our Vision

A nation where street animals are protected by laws and respected by communities, free from hunger, abuse, and preventable suffering.

Core Values

Unconditional compassion, complete financial transparency, relentless rescue action, and community education.

Our Evolution

The NGO Journey Timeline

1
2021 - Humble Beginnings

Small Feeding Drive

Started as a group of 3 passionate animal lovers feeding 20 street dogs daily out of pocket.

2
2022 - Official NGO Registration

Paws Hopes Society Established

Formally registered as a society in India, launching our first mobile rescue vehicle and basic medical facility.

3
2024 - Medical Care Facility

Shelter & OPD Clinic

Expanded shelter capacity to house 100+ recovering animals simultaneously with full-time veterinary doctors.
