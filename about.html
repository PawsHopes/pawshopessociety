About Paws Hopes Society

Our journey, our mission, and our unwavering commitment to street animal welfare in Lucknow.

Our Story

How It All Began

Paws Hopes Society began in 2020, after our founder saw stray dogs eating garbage and drinking dirty drainage water on the streets of Lucknow. Moved by what they witnessed, one person began rescuing and feeding animals — alone, without any organization behind them.

In 2022, that individual effort became an officially registered NGO. Today, our shelter cares for dogs, cats, monkeys, birds, squirrels, and guinea pigs, and we assist with reptile rescues whenever required — carrying the same commitment that started it all: that every animal deserves to know someone cares.

Our Mission

To rescue, treat, shelter, and find loving homes for street animals, while building community awareness and empathy toward them.

Our Vision

A compassionate world where no animal suffers from cruelty, neglect, or preventable disease on the streets.

Core Values

Unconditional compassion, honesty in our work, relentless rescue action, and care for every species — not just dogs and cats.
