Our True History

The Real Journey Behind Paws Hopes Society

Founded out of pure empathy for suffering street animals in Lucknow, driven by action, transparent feeding drives, and medical treatment.

How It All Started

In 2020, Paws Hopes Society was born out of a moment of profound heartbreak. Our founder witnessed hungry street dogs forced to eat discarded plastic garbage and drink black, contaminated drainage water along road margins. Driven by immediate instinct, personal savings were used to prepare fresh home-cooked meals for local strays every single evening.

As word spread and more injured animals were spotted—hit-and-run accident victims, puppies with severe maggot wounds, and abandoned pets—it became clear that feeding alone wasn't enough. These animals desperately required medical intervention, rescue vehicles, antibiotics, and safe shelter.

Official NGO Registration (2022)

To structure rescue operations legally and build a trustworthy platform for donors, Paws Hopes Society was formally registered as an animal welfare organization in India in 2022.

Our Bakshi Ka Talab Shelter Today (2026)

Today in 2026, Paws Hopes Society operates a full shelter located at NH-24, Sitapur Rd, Bakshi Ka Talab, Bhaisamau, Lucknow. We care for dozens of permanent shelter residents and recovering medical cases daily.

Our shelter welcomes species beyond street dogs. We regularly house and treat dogs, cats, monkeys, birds, squirrels, guinea pigs, and actively assist in the safe rescue and relocation of reptiles like snakes.

Our Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, treat, feed, shelter, and find loving homes for abandoned, injured, abused, and neglected animals across Lucknow and surrounding areas.

Our Vision

A compassionate society where no animal suffers silently on the streets from hunger, abuse, or preventable diseases, and where adoption replaces buying.

Core Values

Uncompromising transparency, equal empathy for every species, prompt action during emergencies, and strict financial integrity.
