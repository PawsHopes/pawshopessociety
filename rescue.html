Step-By-Step

Emergency Rescue Protocol

01

Report Location

Call or WhatsApp us with exact Google Maps location and clear photos/videos of the injured animal.

02

Rescue Dispatch

Our nearest volunteer or vehicle is dispatched with first-aid kits and safe transport nets.

03

Vet Treatment

Immediate veterinary examination, wound dressing, blood testing, or emergency surgery support.

04

Shelter Rehab

Transfer to our Bakshi Ka Talab shelter for full nutrition and post-op recovery until adoption.

Real Rescue Transformations

Hit & Run Recovery

Bruno's Orthopedic Miracle

Bruno was found lying paralyzed on Sitapur Road after being struck by a fast truck. After 3 months of orthopedic care, daily laser therapy, and shelter rehab, he runs freely at our sanctuary today.

Canal Rescue

Saving Little Hope From Overflowing Drain

Trapped inside a deep monsoon drain for 18 hours, this young puppy was near starvation. Our team deployed ropes to safely pull her out. She is now healthy and adopted.
