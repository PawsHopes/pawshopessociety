Who We Are

Giving Hope & Healing To Forgotten Street Animals

Paws Hopes Society began in 2020 after our founder saw stray dogs eating garbage and drinking dirty drainage water — and started rescuing and feeding animals alone, caring for approximately 100–150 dogs and cows on the streets. In 2022, that effort became an officially registered NGO. Today our shelter cares for dogs, cats, monkeys, birds, squirrels, and guinea pigs.

Our Vision

A compassionate world where no animal suffers from cruelty, neglect, or preventable disease on the streets.

Our Mission

Rescue, treat, shelter, and find loving forever homes while building awareness and empathy in society.
