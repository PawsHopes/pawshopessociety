Who We Are

Giving a Voice and Refuge to Voiceless Street Animals in Lucknow

Paws Hopes Society is a dedicated animal welfare organization based in Bakshi Ka Talab, Lucknow. Founded in 2020 and officially registered as an Indian NGO, our mission is to rescue injured, sick, and abandoned street animals, provide medical treatment, and offer them a safe sanctuary.

From emergency accident rescues on busy highways like NH-24 to daily feeding drives and foster care programs, our team works tirelessly around the clock because every life matters.
