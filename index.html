Our Story & Mission

From Heartbreak On The Streets To A Full-Fledged Shelter Sanctuary

In 2020, our founder couldn't look away from hungry street animals eating plastic garbage and drinking toxic drainage water. What started as personal feeding drives rapidly grew into Paws Hopes Society, officially registered in 2022.

Today in 2026, we run a sanctuary in Bakshi Ka Talab, Lucknow, providing emergency medical treatment, rehabilitation, daily nutrition, and shelter for dogs, cats, monkeys, birds, squirrels, guinea pigs, and rescued reptiles.
