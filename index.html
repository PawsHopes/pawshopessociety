Who We Are

Giving Hope & Healing To Forgotten Street Animals

Paws Hopes Society is a registered Indian non-profit organization dedicated to animal welfare. Founded with a deep passion for street animals, we operate round-the-clock emergency rescues, medical rehabilitation, sterilization, and adoption drives.

Our Vision

A compassionate world where no animal suffers from cruelty, neglect, or preventable diseases on the streets.

Our Mission

Rescue, treat, shelter, and find loving forever homes while building awareness and empathy in society.
